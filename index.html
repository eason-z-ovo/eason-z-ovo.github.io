Human motion · Machine learning

Fuyan Zhang.

How can familiar motions
adapt to new contexts?

I’m interested in compositional 3D human motion: reusing a specific movement in a new context while preserving what makes it distinctive. My current focus is reference-guided motion composition—how to retain a gesture’s rhythm and form while allowing the rest of the body to adapt.

My background spans efficient deep learning, skeleton-based movement analysis, and spatio-temporal modelling. I have worked on model pruning and knowledge distillation, and studied High Performance Computing with Data Science at the University of Edinburgh and Computing Science at the University of Glasgow.

01 / Current direction

Motion worth keeping.

A familiar gesture.
A different whole-body context.

Imagine taking a particular wave and bringing it into a walk. I want to preserve its timing and expressive shape, while allowing the shoulders, torso, and gait to adjust.

I’m exploring which motion features should remain stable, which should adapt, and how to evaluate both reference fidelity and whole-body coordination on combinations unseen during training.

The longer-term motivation: help animation teams build new behaviours from existing performances, with less manual reworking.
